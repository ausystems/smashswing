Multi-player range mode

Everyone practices at the same time.

Our golf mode works like other simulators. You see your ball flight and your shot data. The big difference: 2 to 6 golfers hit at once.

  • 2 to 6 golfers hit at the same time
  • Each ball flies straight into the screen in front of the player
  • Each player sees their own shot data
  • Range practice, closest to the pin and long drive
