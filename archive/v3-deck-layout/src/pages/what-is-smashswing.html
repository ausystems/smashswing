01What is SmashSwing

Multi-player. Multi-ball tracking.

SmashSwing is a golf entertainment simulator and indoor range. Many golfers hit at the same time on one big screen, and every ball is tracked.

Book a demo Watch the overview

The system

One big screen. Many players. Every ball tracked.

Traditional simulators let one person hit at a time. SmashSwing lets the whole group hit together.

Multi-player

2 to 24 people hit at the same time.

Multi-ball tracking

Every ball is tracked on the same screen, in the same game.

Simulator or indoor range

A 2 to 6 player simulator, or an indoor range for 12 to 24 players.
