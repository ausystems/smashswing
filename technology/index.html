01How it works

Four things happen in the same second.

Launch data for the golfers. Point of contact for the game. One screen, every ball.

01

Every bay fires at one screen

Three to twenty-four hitting stations face a single projection surface, 30 to 100 feet wide.

02

The strike is read, not predicted

Sensors resolve where each ball meets the screen, to the pixel, the instant it lands.

03

The game reacts at that pixel

Asteroids shatter, bricks break, targets explode exactly where the ball hit.

04

Every ball, at once

Dozens of balls in the air are tracked simultaneously. Nobody waits for a turn.
